# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making Agent Skill Kit a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project. Participants should communicate with professionalism, assume good faith while checking evidence, welcome differing technical opinions, and focus criticism on ideas and changes rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct, spam, malicious contribution, and instructions or contributions intended to enable unauthorized harm.

## Maintaining a healthy project

Maintainers may edit, close, or reject contributions that are unsafe, off-topic, deceptive, unlawfully sourced, or inconsistent with the repository’s scope. Security and privacy concerns should be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not disclosed as an exploit demonstration in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository maintainers through the private security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may take any action necessary to protect participants and the project, including warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, or bans.
