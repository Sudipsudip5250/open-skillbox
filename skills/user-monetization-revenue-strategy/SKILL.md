# Monetization and Revenue Strategy

## Quick start

Use this skill when the request matches **Plan or review monetization for websites, apps, content channels, products, or communities. Use for ads, subscriptions, affiliates, sponsorships, paid features, pricing, revenue models, paywalls, or sustainable income strategy.** Start with the smallest defensible input set, state what is known and unknown, then follow the method-selection workflow. Produce an intermediate record before the final answer so another agent can review or continue the work.


## Workflow

1. Define the audience, user problem, channel, content or product value, geography, age profile, traffic quality, distribution, costs, privacy constraints, platform rules, and business objective.
2. Compare models: advertising, subscriptions, one-time purchases, usage-based pricing, freemium, affiliate commissions, sponsorships, marketplace fees, licensing, donations, and services. Evaluate revenue potential, user value, conversion friction, fees, support, churn, fraud, compliance, and maintenance.
3. Choose a primary model and a limited secondary model that fit the product. Design the value exchange, pricing or ad experience, free-versus-paid boundary, cancellation and refund path, disclosure, and support expectations.
4. Create a measurement plan for reach, activation, engagement, conversion, retention, churn, average revenue per user, lifetime value, acquisition cost, ad revenue, refund rate, fraud, and user-experience impact. Define assumptions and confidence.
5. Experiment carefully with pricing, packaging, placement, onboarding, paywalls, offers, and sponsorship formats. Use controlled tests where possible and avoid optimizing a short-term metric at the expense of trust or retention.
6. Reconcile platform fees, payment costs, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, invalid traffic, privacy operations, customer support, content production, and infrastructure before assessing net revenue.

## Rules

- Do not promise guaranteed revenue or call a projection a forecast without assumptions, evidence, and uncertainty.
- Do not use deceptive dark patterns, forced subscriptions, hidden fees, fake urgency, undisclosed sponsorships, invalid ad traffic, or misleading claims.
- Protect user trust: make ads identifiable, explain paid relationships, provide cancellation and refund paths, minimize data collection, and keep core navigation usable.
- Check the current policies of the relevant ad network, app store, payment provider, jurisdiction, and channel before launch.
- Keep regulated tax, consumer-protection, financial, and privacy decisions subject to qualified local review when the facts require it.
