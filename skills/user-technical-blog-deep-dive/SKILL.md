# Technical Blog Deep Dive

## Purpose and scope

Define audience, claim scope, source material, version, practical goal, and what is experience versus generalizable fact.

## Workflow

Frame the problem; explain constraints; show architecture or flow; walk through the key design; compare alternatives; include reproducible examples; discuss failure modes, security, performance, accessibility, and operations; cite official sources; finish with limits and takeaways.

## Verification and quality checks

Verify code and diagrams, version-sensitive claims, benchmarks, citations, terminology, and that examples do not include secrets or imply unsupported production readiness.

## Common errors

Common errors include cargo-cult recommendations, unmeasured performance claims, diagrams that contradict prose, stale APIs, skipping failure paths, and presenting one project’s convention as universal.

## Rules, safety, and non-goals

Do not disclose private architecture, confidential incidents, credentials, or copyrighted material. Label anecdotes, measurements, and sourced facts distinctly. Do not invent sources, data, results, approvals, or completed actions. Preserve privacy and use the smallest relevant skill composition.

## Handoff

Return outline, source register, article, diagrams or examples, verification notes, version caveats, and editorial checklist.
