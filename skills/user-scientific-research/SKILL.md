# Scientific Research

## Workflow

1. Define the question, population or system, intervention or comparison, outcomes, date and language limits, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and intended use of the evidence.
2. Search authoritative databases, primary studies, standards, official datasets, and high-quality reviews. Record query, source, date, version, identifiers, and access limits.
3. Screen evidence for relevance, methods, sample or dataset quality, bias, conflicts, reproducibility, statistical validity, and whether conclusions match the evidence.
4. Extract claims into a structured evidence table with source, method, result, limitation, confidence, and applicability. Separate primary evidence, review evidence, expert opinion, and speculation.
5. Synthesize cautiously. Explain agreement, disagreement, uncertainty, missing data, effect size or practical significance, and what would change the conclusion.
6. Make the analysis reproducible: preserve source references, code or calculation assumptions, data provenance, transformations, and validation checks.
7. Write a report with a direct answer, methods, evidence, limitations, conclusion, and references. Do not turn correlation into causation or preliminary findings into established fact.

## Rules

- Do not fabricate papers, DOIs, datasets, statistics, quotes, citations, or experimental results.
- Prefer primary sources and version-matched official documentation for technical claims. Verify important facts at the source rather than relying on search snippets.
- Do not provide medical, legal, financial, or other regulated personal advice from general research alone; distinguish information from a decision about an individual case.
- Treat unpublished, preprint, generated, and model-produced material as lower-confidence until independently verified.

## Handoff

Report search scope, sources, screening criteria, evidence table, synthesis, limitations, reproducibility artifacts, uncertainty, and references. State which claims are directly supported and which are interpretations.
