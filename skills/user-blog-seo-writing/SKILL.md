# Blog and SEO Writing

## Editorial workflow

1. Define the reader, search intent, business goal, geographic or temporal scope, and desired action.
2. Build a brief with the primary topic, related questions, unique angle, evidence needs, tone, length, and internal-link opportunities.
3. Research the topic and competing content. Identify what readers still need, not merely what competitors repeat.
4. Structure the article around a direct answer, logical headings, scannable sections, useful examples, and a clear conclusion.
5. Write with precise claims, natural language, varied sentence rhythm, and concrete advice. Do not inflate length or hide uncertainty.
6. Add a concise answer near the top, descriptive title and metadata, meaningful headings, internal links, relevant external citations, and descriptive image alt text.
7. Optimize for search without keyword stuffing: use the topic naturally, answer related questions, define terms, and demonstrate first-hand or source-based expertise.
8. Edit for factual accuracy, originality, readability, audience fit, and conversion. Verify every time-sensitive claim before publishing.

## Content quality checks

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| Intent | Does the opening satisfy the reader’s main question quickly? |
| Evidence | Are important claims supported by appropriate sources or clearly labeled as judgment? |
| Usefulness | Does the article help the reader decide or act with examples and trade-offs? |
| Structure | Can a skimming reader understand the argument from headings and tables? |
| Trust | Are author perspective, limitations, dates, and conflicts clear? |
| Search | Are title, headings, entities, links, and metadata natural and descriptive? |
| Originality | Does the piece add analysis, synthesis, examples, data, or a distinct viewpoint? |

Never promise rankings or fabricate statistics, quotations, reviews, user experience, or product capabilities. Use the research module for evidence and the documentation module for publication-ready formatting.


## When not to use this skill

Do not use it to fabricate rankings, reviews, testimonials, first-hand experience, search volume, or product capabilities. Do not replace a scientific, legal, medical, financial, or security workflow when the article depends on specialized evidence or regulated advice.

## Handoff

Report audience and intent, brief, sources and dates, claims requiring verification, article structure, optimization changes, image and alt-text decisions, limitations, and publication checks.
